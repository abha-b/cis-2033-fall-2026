Course overview

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the intuition and implications of fundamental probability and statistics concepts.
  • Select and justify probabilistic models for a given context.
  • Formulate and solve probability problems relevant to computing and other applied fields.

Syllabus

Topics

  • Basic probability, conditional probability, independence, total probability, and Bayes' theorem
  • Counting and combinatorial methods
  • Discrete random variables, common distributions, expectation, and variance
  • Continuous random variables, density and distribution functions, and common distributions
  • Joint distributions, independence, covariance, and correlation
  • The central limit theorem, point estimates, and confidence intervals
